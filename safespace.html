Meet Our Professionals

Meet Our Professionals

Dr. Aanya Mehra

Clinical Psychologist (PsyD),

CBT & Trauma Specialist

I believe healing begins when people feel truly seen

and heard. I strive to create a calm, non-judgmental

space where clients can openly explore their

emotions, experiences, and inner conflicts.


After completing my Doctorate in Clinical Psychology,

I began integrating Cognitive Behavioural Therapy

(CBT) with trauma-informed practices to support

emotional healing and resilience.


My therapeutic approach is compassionate,

collaborative, and insight-oriented. I work

closely with clients to help them understand

recurring emotional patterns, regulate

overwhelming feelings, and build healthier

coping mechanisms.


I believe therapy is not about “fixing” someone,

but about helping individuals reconnect with

themselves with greater clarity, confidence,

and self-compassion.

Anxiety & Relationship Counsellor

I am deeply passionate about helping people

There's no fixed timeline — it depends entirely on you, your goals, and what you're working through. Some people feel a shift in a few sessions. Others work for months. We go at your pace, not a programme's.
